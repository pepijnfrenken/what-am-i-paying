# UK income tax 2026/27 — calculator source (income-tax route only)

Verified 2026-10-01 on **gov.uk/income-tax-rates** ("Income Tax rates and Personal
Allowances", tax year 6 April 2026 – 5 April 2027). The "Where does my money go"
route shows **income tax only — NI explicitly excluded** (labelled in the view).

## Thresholds / bands (England, Wales, NI)

| Band | Taxable income | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Personal Allowance | up to £ 12,570 | 0 % |
| Basic rate | £ 12,571 – £ 50,270 (bands sit on **total income**; the PA carves the bottom of the 20% band, so a tapered PA widens it to 50,270 − PA) | 20 % |
| Higher rate | £ 50,271 – £ 125,140 | 40 % |
| Additional rate | over £ 125,140 | 45 % |

- **PA taper:** allowance reduces by £1 per £2 of (adjusted net) income above
  £ 100,000 → zero at £ 125,140 or above.
- **Employee NI (2026/27):** 8 % between £ 12,570 and £ 50,270, 2 % above
  (annualised main-rate band) — **excluded** from this calculator's route;
  the label says so. Source: gov.uk/national-insurance-rates-letters.
- No reductions for blind person's allowance, marriage allowance,
  Scotland-specific bands, savings/dividend allowances.

Sources: https://www.gov.uk/income-tax-rates (updated 2026-10-01); 2026/27 bands
frozen at 2021/22 levels per Autumn Budget 2025 (gov.uk/government/publications).
