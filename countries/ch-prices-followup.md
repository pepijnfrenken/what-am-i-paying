## Uncertainties / flags

- Migros / Coop webshops are JS-rendered and could not be scraped; the sourced prices are from Denner (supermarket), Manor, OTTO'S and drinks specialists. Migros/Coop are expected to fall within ±5–10% of these.
- No official average-price series exists for spirits (unlike fuel or electricity); all figures are regular (non-promo) shelf/webshop prices. Chains run frequent 25–50% spirits promotions, so a single visit can be far below the band.
- Smirnoff exists as 37.5% and 40% ABV variants (small price differences); Absolut price skew can come from parallel imports.
- Prices checked 2026-10-05; alcohol shelf prices are sticky, but promo churn is weekly — re-check before publication.
