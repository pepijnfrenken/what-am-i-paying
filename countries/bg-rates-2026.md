# BG rates 2026 — sources for the calculator

Researched 2026-10-05 (NRA/НАП, ЗАДС via customs.bg, lex.bg, minfin.bg, НСИ-adjacent checks).
**Bulgaria adopted the euro on 1 January 2026 — fixed rate 1.95583 BGN = 1 EUR.** Alcohol/energy excises are still written in BGN in ЗАДС and apply as EUR at the fixed rate (ЗВЕРБ чл. 11–13); tobacco rates are written directly in EUR (State Budget Act 2026, in force 1.8.2026). All money below in EUR; excises are VAT-exclusive (20% VAT on top).

## ДДС (VAT)

| Rate | Applies to |
|---|---|
| 20% standard | Everything else — incl. general food, bread, clothes, electronics, restaurant & catering (their temporary 9% ended 31.12.2024) |
| 9% reduced | Hotels/camping accommodation; books/textbooks/newspapers/magazines (paper & e-); baby foods, nappies, baby hygiene (ЗДДС Annex 4) |
| 0% | Exports / intra-EU supplies only |

Source: https://nra.bg/wps/portal/nra/taxes/dds-v-balgariya

## Данъци върху дохода (income side, 2026)

| Item | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Personal income tax (ДОД) | **10% flat** | From the first euro, on gross minus employee contributions; no tax-free allowance |
| Employee social & health contributions | **13.78%** of gross | ДОО 8.38% (pensions 6.58 + ОЗМ 1.40 + unemployment 0.40) + ДЗПО 2.20% + ЗО 3.20%. Born before 1.1.1960: 11.58% |
| Maximum insurance income (ceiling) | **€ 2,300.00 /month** | Aug–Dec 2026 (Jan–Jul 2026: €2,111.64). Above: no contributions, only 10% tax |
| Minimum wage (floor) | € 620.20 /month | From 1.1.2026 |
| **Effective marginal — up to the ceiling** | **≈ 22.40%** | = 13.78% + 10% × (1 − 13.78%) |
| **Effective marginal — above the ceiling** | **10.00%** | Contributions capped |

**Calculator rule — annual ДОД (single employee):** tax = 10% × (gross − contributions),
contributions = 13.78% × gross capped at the insurance ceiling. The mid-year change
(Jan–Jul 2026: € 2,111.64/month; Aug–Dec 2026: € 2,300.00/month) is handled
pro-rata by month count: annual cap = 7 × 2,111.64 + 5 × 2,300.00 = **€ 26,281.48**.
Applicable for the whole tax year 2026; a full-year view at a single figure would
overstate (5 × (2,300 − 2,111.64) = € 941.80 of insurable income).

Sources: minfin.bg/bg/827 (ЗДДФЛ чл. 48); https://nra.bg (осигурителен доход); bulsmetka.bg (НАП/КСО split).

## Uncertainties / flags

- ракия draft law (public consultation to 23.10.2026) proposes fines for holding another person's home rakiya — not law yet.
- Finance minister quoted spirits €565.42/hl once; the Act (authoritative) says BGN 1,100 = €562.42.
- Cigarette rates changed 1.8.2026 (mid-year); Jan–Jul 2026 used older figures.
- LPG/CNG zero rates expire 31.12.2026 — date-stamp any view.
- Restaurant/services VAT is 20% again (some guides still list 9%).
- No reduced VAT for bread/general food — only baby goods among foodstuffs.
