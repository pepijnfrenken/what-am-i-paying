## Uncertainties / flags

- Draft beer is a single Sofia venue menu check (order.bg) — venue spread is wide (€3.40–5.40 typical, €6.50 outlier in a premium bar); treat 4.00 as a Sofia bar default, not a national average.
- Supermarket beer €1.00 is a mid default: regular singles run €0.90–1.30, promo/multipack units fall to €0.43–0.79; NSI's average paid price (€0.96/L) is pack-weighted.
- Wine €5.00 is an explicit estimate — shelf spread €1.29 (promo) to €7+; budget lines dominate at €3–4.50; aggregator "min price" can mix pack sizes.
- Cigarettes: no single official October-2026 average; €3.95 = July average (€3.80) plus the 1.8.2026 excise step (+≈ €0.13–0.16). Check per-brand registered prices in the Customs BACIS registry before launch.
- Fuel: fuelo averages move daily (Sept–Oct 2026 was volatile: diesel peaked at €1.96); values are 2026-10-01.
- Electricity: quoted Sofia (Electrohold) incl. network + 20% VAT; other suppliers/tariff zones differ ~±3% (night ≈ €0.092); national incl.-tax average was €0.1355/kWh (Eurostat H2 2025) and KEVR compensations can shift the effective price slightly.
- Rakia: comparison minima may mix pack sizes/promos (observed €4.91–7.39); the Sunrise (Сънрайз) brand was not separately priced — Peshtera used as the standard reference.
- Bread: producers had flagged up to +10% (to ≈ €1.20) as of mid-Sept 2026 — values may be one step higher if that passes.
- All BGN figures converted at the fixed 1.95583 rate; dual-label rounding can differ by a cent.
