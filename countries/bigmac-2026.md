## Uncertainties

- UK could have moved to £5.99 in-store by late 2026 (delivery trackers suggest it); £5.49 is the best-sourced in-store figure (Economist Jul-2026).
- NL has no enforced national price (franchise advice pricing); €6.10 = typical, Amsterdam is higher.
- NL's 9 % restaurant rate has been debated politically (nothing enacted as of Oct 2026).
- BG price verified mainly via ordering platforms + a news check; in-store Sofia runs a few cents lower.
- All prices are typical walk-in menu prices, not app-deal transaction prices.
