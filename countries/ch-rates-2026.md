## Uncertainties / flags

- **VAT increase pending (NOT in force):** Parliament decided June 2026 to raise the standard rate 8.1→8.5 % (reduced untouched) for the 13th AHV pension — constitutional, needs a referendum. Keep 8.1/2.6/3.8 until then.
- Zürich Steuerfuss: adopted 95 % (Kantonsrat), but a Feb-2026 withholding sheet still shows 98 % — one confirmation recommended before launch.
- Geneva combined row uses the fiscal-2025 base barème (latest official); 2026 cold-progression indexing may shift thresholds slightly.
- Cantonal/communal multipliers differ by commune (GE communes 25–51 centimes etc.); examples are for the named cities only.
- Wine: cantonal monopoly fees not quantified.
- Fuel prices at record highs (Sept–Oct 2026: Cressier outage, low Rhine).
- Federal tariff quirk: single-person top marginal is 13.2 % up to ~793,900, then flat 11.5 % above.
